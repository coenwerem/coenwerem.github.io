Coordinated Manipulation Through Sustained Contact

Dexterous bimanual manipulation requires coordinated motion and sustained contact as objects move, interact, and are released. We study articulated door opening, part insertion, and constrained object relocation using the A7-L6 bimanual robot manipulator. The demonstrations below present task execution in MuJoCo.
