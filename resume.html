Summary

Doctoral candidate in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland researching risk-aware dexterous grasping and manipulation under uncertainty, spanning grasp synthesis, belief estimation, and certified execution. Experienced in designing and implementing robust grasp planning and control algorithms for robotic arms with anthropomorphic hands, validated through high-fidelity simulation (MuJoCo, Drake, Isaac Sim) and real hardware (Fairino FR3, xArm7, LEAP Hand v1, RealHand L6). Seeking research engineering, applied ML, or robotics roles.

Research Software

  • GDF — Planner-free certified grasp execution. A stationary feedback law descends a softmin grasp distance field under a CBF-CLF quadratic program, lifting 46 of 50 objects while holding a median 94% of the synthesized quality margin at 0.09 ms solve time per 20 ms control step. (Python, MuJoCo, CasADi) Project page
  • EquiDexFlow — SE(3)-equivariant flow-matching model generating wrist pose, joint angles, contacts, and forces from an object point cloud, with friction-cone compliance by construction. Zero friction violations and a 0.46 Nm wrench residual over 8,100 certified grasps; retargets to a physical LEAP Hand through IK alone. (Python, PyTorch) Project page · GitHub
  • VNB-Grasp — Differentiable Gaussian-mixture belief over contact parameters and object pose yielding pathwise CVaR gradients, packaged with a MuJoCo benchmark that scores friction sensitivity and perturbation tolerance. (Python, PyTorch, MuJoCo) GitHub
  • Imitation Learning for Grasp Stabilization — Trained neural policies (MLP, LSTM, DP3) via behavior cloning from expert demonstrations to stabilize grasps under perturbations using tactile and proprioceptive observations. (Python, PyTorch, MuJoCo)
